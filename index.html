Powerups & Rules

Runners see everyone simply as players. Taggers can see who is a tagger and who is a runner.

👻 Ghost Trail Hides your real player from everyone else and sends a moving false location instead. Long duration.
👥 Clone Keeps your real location visible while spawning multiple moving decoys heading in different directions. Short duration.
📡 Radar Scanner Temporarily reveals everyone's live location to you. During the reveal, player roles are also revealed to you.
🫥 Invisibility Removes your player from everyone else's map for a limited time.
💊 Antidote A gamble. 50% chance to cure a tagger into a runner, 50% chance to infect a runner and turn them into a tagger.
🎯 Hunt For taggers. Finds the closest runner, marks them prominently and displays their distance. Other taggers can see the hunted player too.
🚧 Sabotage Temporarily creates a restricted area inside the playable boundary.
🚆 Train Sabotage Temporarily disables the selected train line(s).

Powerups spawn randomly around the map. Tap one to see what it is, how long it remains available, and activate it.
